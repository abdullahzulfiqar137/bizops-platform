Recommended Business Operational Workflow

Follow these 5 steps to maximize your operational efficiency using BizOps Enterprise:

1

Business & Team Setup

Configure company details in Business Profile and grant staff accounts with custom roles in Users & Access.

2

Catalog & Initial Stock

Setup product Categories, add Products with SKU & pricing, then record initial levels via Stock Adjustments.

3

Entity Directories

Add key business contacts into Customers CRM and vendor records in Suppliers Directory for fast invoicing.

4

Daily Operations

Process customer Sales & Invoices, issue Purchase Orders to vendors, track Payments & record daily Expenses.

5

Intelligence & Reporting

Monitor profit margins in Financial Reports, review automated ledger entries in Accounting, and consult AI Insights.
